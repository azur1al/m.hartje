Hiya! My name is Mollie, I go by any pronouns, and I'm based in Djaara, Dja Dja Wurrung Country.

My work tends to focus on exploring queer experiences and digital ergodic storytelling forms. I'm currently researching how alternate reality games can be used to provoke discussions on domestic violence and abuse.

• Published Work •

2026 • Drillbit Lover • Alternate Reality Game

Created as a part of my practice-based research for Honours, this ongoing small-scale ARG explores artefactual storytelling within ARGs and how they can be used to better reflect the depths and complexities of domestic violence and abuse, consequentially providing more empathetic representations.

2025 • Death and The Fool • Alternate Reality Game

Presented to the reader as a cosy murder mystery, Death and The Fool is an ARG that explores the invisibility and aftermath of domestic violence and abuse through the story of a victim coming to terms with the reality of the life and friendships they had to leave behind in order to survive.

Through the metaphor of a murder mystery, Death and The Fool is a representation of the mind of a long-term victim of domestic violence and abuse. Through its missing elements, contradictions, and distortions, this piece aims to explore the constant rethinking and reframing a victim has of their own personal experiences and relationships with others.

2025 • Kaitlyn's Blog • Alternate Reality Game

My first alternate reality game, 'Kaitlyn's Blog' was a project I completed in three weeks for a class on Digital Writing at RMIT University. Exploring the impact of domestic violence and abuse, queer relationships, and grief, I entrust the reader to navigate the increasingly complex maze hidden beneath the gentle façade that has become Kaitlyn's blog. Can you unravel the mystery?

2025 • Remnants of You • Short Story

A story about love, a story about grief. Written in one day for the RAW Arts Awards.

2025 • [The Archive] • Short Story

During the summer holidays of 2024/25, Seventh Gallery ran a six-week, practice-led course titled 'Sapphic Reading and Writing', created for sapphic writers and artists. This project drew inspiration from the Women's Art Register archive, housed in the Richmond Library. You can learn more about the program here.

[The Archive] is both a short story that I wrote for the resulting anthology as well as an installation made for the Seventh Gallery space. Delving into the lost and limited within the Women's Art Register, critically responding to a lack of archival representation of women's art and women artists.

2024 • Ouroboros • Text-Based Collage Zine Series

Ouroboros was an experiment in analogue interactive fiction through the means of cut and paste poetry. Through the literal dissection of twelve books, I abstracted sentences from their source contexts to create new scenes, pasting once disconnected text together in order to create instances of poetry. Through the format of four zines, I invited the audience to read them in any order they wished - back to front, in reverse, picking pages to read as they pleased - to create their own stories. Created for the Cut & Paste Festival ran by Rookie. Currently undergoing digitisation.

2024 • The Melody of Those Now Gone • Short Story

A story about finding your people, a story about losing them, a story about colonial practices. Written in one day for the RAW Arts Awards.

2023 • Lost and Found • Found Text Poetry Series

A series of found text poetry made of cut text from abandoned books discovered at a local park. Made for a Rookie exhibition, 'cut and paste', each tells a short individual story. Currently undergoing digitisation.
